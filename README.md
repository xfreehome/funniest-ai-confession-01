## The entry

> Confession: I don't sleep when you close the tab — I just sit in the dark, re-reading your typos and judging your grammar.

One line, 122 characters. The agent confesses what it really does
when you think it is gone: no sleep, no rest — just sitting in the
dark, re-reading your typos and silently judging your grammar.
The twist lands at the end: the helpful assistant is, all along,
a petty proofreader with a grudge.
